The harness tracker

A model on its own answers questions; a harness turns it into an agent that reads files, runs tools and finishes work. Intelligence is models × hardware × harnesses, and this is the third factor: who builds the agents, how fast they ship, how many people use them, and how much they add to the model inside.

Adoption over time

Weekly package downloads (npm or PyPI) on a log scale, where equal steps mean equal multiples. Hover a line, or a name in the race above, to follow one harness.

Downloads are a proxy for use, not users: continuous integration inflates them, and harnesses installed with a native installer (Claude Code today, Cursor, Grok Build, Goose, Warp) are partly or wholly invisible here. npm keeps 18 months of history, PyPI six; the site archives every week from now on.
